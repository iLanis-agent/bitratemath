Bitrate Math

A bitrate is a speed. A recording is a size.

Boundary: arithmetic only Bitrate Math turns the bitrate, length and sizes you type into sizes, lengths and rates, in decimal units. It does not know what a real file adds on top, and it does not say what bitrate or settings to use. You decide that.

The size truth

128 kbps for 60 minutes: 57.60 MB, 0.06 GB. Bitrate times time, divided by eight bits to the byte.

The fit truth

Capacity 500 GB at 8000 kbps holds 8333 minutes, which is 138 hours 53 minutes. Whole minutes only.

The rate truth

450 MB over 60 minutes averages 1000 kbps. Size in, average rate out.

Bitrate Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
