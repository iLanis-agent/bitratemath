Bitrate Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page turns the bitrate, length and sizes you type into sizes, lengths and rates, in decimal units (1 MB is 1,000,000 bytes, 1 GB is 1000 MB). It does not know what a real file adds on top, and it does not say what bitrate or settings to use.

The size truth

How big a recording is at a steady bitrate and length.

The fit truth

How many whole minutes a capacity holds at a steady bitrate.

The rate truth

The average bitrate of a file whose size and length you know.
